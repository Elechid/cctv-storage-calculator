How much storage do four CCTV cameras need for 30 days?

At 4 Mbps per camera, four cameras recording continuously for 30 days need about 5.184 TB before headroom. With 20% extra, plan for about 6.221 TB of usable storage. This uses decimal TB and an average bitrate. Check the recorder’s supported drives, formatting and any RAID overhead.
